Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2025

1. Overview

This portfolio project is a non-commercial, educational demo. It does not use a server and does not collect personal data on a backend. All interactions occur locally in your browser.

2. Data Controller

The provider identified on the Imprint page is the contact for privacy matters related to this demo.

3. What data is stored

  • Player name
  • Score details
  • Audio settings

These values are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. No identifiers are used to track you across sites or devices.

4. Purpose and legal basis

Purpose: to enable basic gameplay features such as showing your name on the leaderboard, remembering scores, and restoring audio preferences. Legal basis under GDPR: legitimate interests of the project owner in operating an educational demo and your consent implied by using optional features.

5. No cookies, tracking, or third parties

  • No cookies or tracking pixels
  • No analytics services
  • No third-party embeds that process personal data

6. Storage duration

Data in local storage remains on your device until you clear it or reset the game data. Deleting browser data removes these entries.

7. Your controls

  • You can clear browser data or local storage in your browser settings.
  • If the game provides a reset option, it will remove stored entries created by the demo.

8. International transfers

No international data transfers occur because no server-side processing is performed.

9. Children

This demo is intended for general audiences. If you are under 13, use it only with permission from a parent or guardian.

10. Your rights

Under applicable law (including GDPR), you may have rights such as access, rectification, deletion, objection, and complaint to a supervisory authority. Because data is stored only locally on your device, exercising these rights typically means deleting the local storage entries. For questions, use the contact details on the Imprint page.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect improvements to the demo or legal requirements. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.
